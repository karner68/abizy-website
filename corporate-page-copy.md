## What a Partnership Looks Like

**Eyebrow:** What a partnership looks like

## More than a logo on a page

A Corporate Partnership keeps your company in front of Argyle's business community all year — in our channels, on our stage, and in the room at our biggest events. Here's what that looks like in practice:

### Year-round brand presence

Your logo and name stay in front of local businesses all year — on argylebiz.com, in the ABA member newsletter, and across our social media. Not a one-time shout-out; a standing presence as an official ABA Corporate Partner.

### A stage in front of local leaders

Guaranteed speaking slots at ABA Academy Lunch & Learns and Weekly Trainings — real time in front of decision-makers to share your expertise and become a go-to name in your field, not just a logo on a banner.

### A reserved seat at the Big Bash

Reserved tables at Argyle's biggest night of the year — eight seats at the Business level, sixteen at Signature — so your team and your guests are in the room for the celebration, included at no extra cost.

### A real relationship with the ABA

First right of refusal on signature-event sponsorships, and — at the Signature level — a dedicated annual check-in with ABA leadership to align on community goals and grow the partnership over time.

## Partnership Levels

**Eyebrow:** Choose your level

## Three ways to partner

Every level is an annual partnership and builds on the one before it. Pick the fit for your company's goals and budget.

### Community Partner

**$4,500/year**

- Recognized as an official ABA Corporate Partner — logo and recognition on ABA's website, newsletter, and social media, year-round
- Standing invitation to all ABA networking events and Friday Connect
- One guaranteed speaking slot per year at an ABA Academy Lunch & Learn or Weekly Training

**Action:** Become a Community Partner

### Business Partner

**$7,500/year**

- Everything in Community Partner
- A reserved table (8 tickets) at Big Bash each year, included at no additional cost
- Two guaranteed speaking slots per year at ABA Academy Lunch & Learn or Weekly Training
- First right of refusal to upgrade to a Presenting-level sponsorship on any signature event before it opens to other prospects

**Action:** Become a Business Partner

### Signature Partner

**$10,500/year**

- Everything in Business Partner
- Two reserved tables (16 tickets) at Big Bash each year
- Priority pick of Lunch & Learn date and topic each year
- Recognized as a Founding Signature Partner, with more prominent placement across ABA marketing than standard Partner recognition
- A dedicated annual check-in with ABA leadership to align on community goals and where the partnership can grow

**Action:** Become a Signature Partner
